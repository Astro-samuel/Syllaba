# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students managing coursework across multiple classes — importing syllabi, tracking deadlines, checking a calendar, and projecting grades. Used in short, frequent sessions (checking what's due today, logging a grade after an exam) rather than long deep-work sessions. Low tolerance for friction; they're here to get in, check status, get out.

## Product Purpose

Syllaba turns a pasted or uploaded syllabus into a structured timeline, calendar, and grade tracker automatically. It exists as a 100% free, open-source alternative to Upahead — same job, no paywall, no login required, everything stored locally. Success is a student trusting the parsed schedule enough to stop checking the original syllabus PDF.

## Brand Personality

Calm, organized, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a well-kept planner, not a marketing site — quiet confidence rather than gamified urgency. Playful touches (streaks, confetti) are earned rewards, not the dominant tone.

## Anti-references

Not cluttered or childish edtech UI (busy gradients, cartoon mascots, notification-bait). Not a glowing "hacker dashboard" dark-neon aesthetic — that reads as a dev tool, not a study tool. Not a dense enterprise dashboard with wall-to-wall data tables.

## Design Principles

- Match Upahead's core interaction model (sidebar nav, month calendar, timeline-first dashboard) since this is positioned as its free alternative — students switching over should feel at home immediately.
- Legibility over decoration: this is read constantly under time pressure (before class, during exam week).
- One calm palette used consistently — the accent pastels mark category/type, they don't decorate for their own sake.
- Every screen answers "what's due, and when" within the first glance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast minimum for all body text and UI labels. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No color-only signaling (course/type badges pair color with text, not color alone).
